⚔️ STAR WARS BATTLE ARENA ⚔️
============================

A turn-based battle game where you face off against iconic Star Wars characters!

## 🎮 Game Features

- **4 Enemy Levels**: Battle progressively harder opponents:
  - 🪖 Stormtrooper (Easy)
  - 🤠 Han Solo (Medium)
  - 🎯 Boba Fett (Hard)
  - 🖤 Darth Vader (Legendary)

- **Action System**: 4 unique moves per turn:
  - ⚔️ **ATTACK**: Basic attack dealing 15 damage
  - 🛡️ **DEFEND**: Reduce incoming damage
  - ⚡ **SPECIAL**: Powerful critical hit (2.5x damage)
  - 💊 **HEAL**: Restore 30 health points

- **RPG Progression**: 
  - Level up after each victory
  - Increased attack power
  - Full health restoration between battles

- **Dynamic Combat**:
  - Damage variance ±5
  - Enemy AI with defense logic
  - Special attacks and healing
  - Real-time battle log

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Choose your action each turn:
   - **ATTACK**: Deal normal damage to the enemy
   - **DEFEND**: Reduce incoming damage (5 armor)
   - **SPECIAL**: Deal 2.5x damage with risk/reward
   - **HEAL**: Restore health but lose a turn

3. Defeat all 4 enemies to win the game
4. Each enemy defeated increases your level and attack power

## 📊 Game Balance

### Starting Stats
- **Player**: 100 HP, 15 ATK, 1 Level
- **Enemies**: Progressively stronger

### Enemy Stats
| Enemy | HP | ATK | Special |
|-------|----|----|---------|
| Stormtrooper | 80 | 12 | Rapid Fire |
| Han Solo | 100 | 18 | Quick Draw |
| Boba Fett | 120 | 22 | Thermal Detonator |
| Darth Vader | 150 | 28 | Force Choke |

## 💡 Strategy Tips

1. **Balance offense and defense**: Don't always attack
2. **Use Special wisely**: Great for finishing weakened enemies
3. **Watch health**: Heal before you get too low
4. **Study enemy patterns**: Each opponent has different tactics
